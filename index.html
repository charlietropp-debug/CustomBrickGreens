    Questions

    Is it real LEGO?

    Yes. All of our bricks are real, genuine LEGO.

    Can we see it before we pay?

    Yes. Send us photos of the building and we'll put together a mock-up so you know what the model will look like.

    What do we receive?

    The pieces, shipped to you, and a step-by-step instruction PDF sent to your email. Your team builds it. We're happy to send a sample instruction PDF first.

    Is the price flexible?

    Somewhat. If you have a budget or want more than one building, just ask and we'll work with you.

    What photos do you need?

    The building from the front, back, left and right, plus any other angles you like. A 360 video walking around the outside is great too.
